## A few more plain facts

- Tapeline adds up the areas you draw. It does not decide what counts as gross living area, and it does not apply or check any measuring standard (for example ANSI Z765) or any lender, client or government guideline. Those decisions are yours.
- Square footage and totals are shown rounded to one decimal place. Totals are added up before rounding, so a total can differ by 0.1 sf from adding up the rounded area labels yourself.
- Nothing you draw is sent anywhere. Your sketches stay on your computer, in the files you save. "Recovery copies" are kept only in your own web browser and can be lost if the browser's data is cleared. Always save a project file.
- If you change the program, or use a copy someone else changed, the changed copy is your responsibility. Run the built-in self-tests (the "Run Self-Tests" button) after any change.
